# Maxsen price catalogue (reference)

Transcribed from the client's price catalogue PDF (_Catalog_Newest_1_compressed.pdf_, 8 pages,
My Digital Lock × Maxsen), which is image-only so prices can't be read from it automatically. **Check
prices here before changing any default in `packages/domain/src/pricing/pricing.ts` or
`packages/domain/src/sample/catalogue.ts`.** Prices are S$, "U.P." is the usual price shown crossed out.
